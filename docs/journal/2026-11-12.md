## Batch: the launcher starts mega without the operator's environment (implementation agent)

Built to `docs/future/roadmap/the-launcher-starts-mega-without-the-operators-environment.md`; the card's state is unchanged.

### What was built

- `cmd/mega/desktop.go`: `desktopLaunch(executable, shell, plain)` builds the `Exec` line. For bash, zsh or fish, named by an absolute `SHELL`, it is `"<shell>" -i -c "exec '<mega>' daemon"`. The binary is quoted for the shell by `shellQuote` (POSIX `'\''`, fish `\'` and `\\`), then the script and the shell path are quoted by ranger's `desktopExecPath`. Anything else falls back to the direct form with a printed warning; `--plain` asks for the direct form. `integrate` prints the `Exec` line and one sentence on why.
- `internal/harness`: `Check()` joins the `Harness` interface, with `MissingError{Harness, Executable, Reason}` (`Is(ErrExecutableMissing)`) and `FindExecutable`, which uses `LookPath` for a bare name and stats a path. Claude checks `node`, then `script()` as "the claude shim". Codex checks its binary. The fake has `SetMissing` and `FailHistory`.
- `internal/service/preflight.go`:
  - `preflight()` gives `{Path, Missing}`, sorted by harness, on every `Workspace()` read;
  - `harnessReady` refuses with `CodeExecutableMissing`, `executable_missing` (409), in Prompt, Start and StartConversation, after the gate and checkout checks;
  - `explain(harness, err)` maps a `MissingError`, or any failure while `Check` fails, to that code. It wraps the importHistory error (Prompt to a stopped conversation, Start, Resume, Adopt), Discover, Models and the startRuntime errors.
- The wire field is `preflight {path, missing: [{harness, executable, reason}]}`. `reason` is the full `MissingError` text.
- The UI: `LaunchDialog` takes `unavailable` and `path`. A harness that cannot run is `disabled`, marked "(cannot run)", with a notice naming the reason and mega's `PATH`. Launch carries the reason, and the initial harness is the first that can run.
- `start()` logs `PATH at start-up: '…'` once.

## Batch: opening a conversation costs its whole history (implementation agent)

Built to `docs/future/roadmap/opening-a-conversation-costs-its-whole-history.md`; the card's state is unchanged. Out of scope, and not built: on-demand raw payloads, loading on scroll, skipping off-screen rendering, start-up, compaction.

### What was built

- **`internal/events/index.go`.** The in-memory index: a seek point every 256 events and every turn start, built by `Open`'s scan, kept by `Append`, never written.
  - Its turn rule: the first event, the event after each `turn_end`, and an imported prompt (`user` of origin `native`) once the turn before holds content and no call waits for a result. Such a turn starts at the first event after the last content.
  - `Range(from, to, keep)` seeks, and `Since` is `Range`. `TurnsBefore(seq, n)` returns the stretch's start, the turns before it and the newest seq, under one lock.
- **`Service.History`.** It takes `HistoryQuery{Turns, Before, Since, Native}` and gives `History{Events, From, Through, Windowed, EarlierTurns}`.
  - `Events` stays the whole-log read the tests and e2e use.
  - `Since(0)` in the service's own open, recovery and fresh-session checks is unchanged; start-up has its own card.
- **The route.** `GET …/events` takes `turns`, `before`, `since` and `native`, and answers `{events, from, through, earlier?}`. A request over a second is logged by `slowRequests` in `internal/server/slow.go`; the stream is excluded.
- **The page.**
  - `log.ts` holds the stretch `{from, through, earlier, native, loaded, ahead}`, with `opened`, `earlier`, `caughtUp`, `received`, `withNative` and `withoutNative`. `loadedThrough` is gone. The reading position posts `through`.
  - The opening read retries with backoff. Show earlier and Show all are `IconButton`s with words (`arrow_upward`, `vertical_align_top`).
  - The view hold measures the first row in view by `data-seq`. An action group is keyed by its first event's seq instead of its index, so prepending does not remount groups.
- **Diagnostics.** `waiting.ts` and `useWallNow`, a clock that does not freeze while stale. `useStream` returns `{state, waiting}`. The connecting view lists the stream and workspace waits, the footer's connection label has the wait as its title, and the loading line names the request.
