## Adoption reaches any session in the checkout (implementation agent)

`adoption-reaches-any-session-in-the-checkout` was built from the card alone. It is in the working tree, not committed. The card's state and `.ranger/` are untouched.

**The two things found out first.**

- **The SDK's `listSessions` has no cap of its own** (SDK 0.3.318). The shim's `list` returned 436 rows for `claude-rt1` in 661 ms, the same ids as its 436 session files. A privacy hook refused reads of the minified SDK bundle, so this rests on the count and the type file.
- **Codex's `thread/list` pages, but not as the card expected** (codex-cli 0.166.0). It reached the end by `nextCursor`. It also returns at most 100 a page, and under its default sort, `created_at`, a page that ends inside a second skips the rest of that second. The cursor is a whole-second time. Under `updated_at` the cursor carries milliseconds, and nothing was missed at page sizes 1, 2 and 3 across the fixture's one tie of two. The work stopped there and went to Michael.
- **Michael's ruling:** page by `updated_at`, 100 a page, to the end, dropping an id already seen. A thread updated during the read is left: "nothing is built for it; reopening the dialog reads the list again." And no page bound: the loop runs under discovery's one-minute deadline, so a list that never ends fails with an error. I checked that the deadline reaches each page's call before building: `conn.wait` selects on the context.
- **The fixture's full list is 344,** matching its interactive rollouts on disk, 342 `vscode` and 2 `cli`. One `exec` rollout is left out by Codex's default `sourceKinds`, as before. The whole list takes 1.0 to 1.3 s in pages of 50 to 100, against the minute.

**The shape.**

- **Codex.** `Discover` dials once and calls `thread/list {cwd, limit: 100, sortKey: updated_at, cursor}` until `nextCursor` is empty, with a set of seen ids. `discoverLimit` is gone, and `listPage` and `listSort` say why they are what they are.
- **pi.** `discoverLimit` and `discover`'s `limit` are gone.
- **The page.** The adopt dialog's filter matches the id as well as the first prompt. Its label, placeholder and empty line say so.
- **Unchanged:** `internal/service`, the routes, the Claude adapter and the shim.
