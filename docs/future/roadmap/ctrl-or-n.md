let's just chop that to `ctrl+/`.

let's also use `ctrl+'` to show/hide the side panel.

## shape

- **Next waiting is Ctrl+/ only.** The bare `n` binding goes. The needs-you section's hint reads "Ctrl+/", and its tooltip loses the second half.
- **Ctrl+' shows and hides the side panel,** switching between the panel and the rail, from anywhere including the reply box. The collapse and expand controls name it in their titles.
- Both are window-level and never steal a keystroke that would type into a field: each requires Ctrl.
- Alt chords are still not used; this browser takes them before the page sees them (journal, 2026-11-09). Ctrl+' should be confirmed to reach the page in Michael's browser the way Ctrl+/ was, with a key logger in the console, before it is relied on.
