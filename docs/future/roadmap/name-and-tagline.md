instead of "a workspace for a gang of coding agents"... we should come up with something better.

and instead of saying "metawoo mega" as the page title... it should just say "mega".

## what the tagline is about

Michael, 2026-11-12: mega is about the practice even more than about coordinating agents. It is the wedge that refines and streamlines the whole practice into what orienteering can be for a solo operator. So the line should speak from inside that figure, as the grimoire's `agents/orienteering` defines it: an operator navigating by map-to-ground correspondence, with their own map and compass and not a platform's turn-by-turn, never running long on a stale bearing. The agents are the gang the method already assumes, not the subject.

House register, for comparison: ranger "your roadmap lives in your repo", mercurius "mediates the design-build duel", console "the shape of a good console". Short, lowercase, what the thing is for.

## decision (2026-11-12, provisional)

**build your constellation.** Michael's choice for now, with an asterisk: he may revisit it, and the point of this card is only to replace what is there with something better. It fits on two counts. The constellation is his own word for the body of work the practice produces, and the hub mark already draws one, points around a centre. Keep the tagline in one constant on the Go side and one on the UI side, so revisiting it is a two-line change.

## candidates considered

- **keep your bearing** (the planning agent's pick): the daily meaning and the method's meaning in two words; reads without knowing the practice.
- **your own map and compass**: the orienteering note's phrase for what separates this from a platform.
- **orienteering, run from one place**: names the practice outright; depends on the reader knowing the word.
- **navigate, don't delegate**: the hub principle, mega carries and never decides, as a slogan.

Wherever there is room, the short line is followed by a sentence naming the practice: mega is where a solo operator runs an orienteering practice with a gang of agents.

## where it appears

- The page title becomes `mega`, with the needs-you count and the shutdown state prefixing it as now, and the notification title likewise.
- The tagline: the launcher's comment, the root command's one-line help, the README's opening, the header of `docs/current/mega.md`, and the description on the GitHub repository, which only Michael can set.
- "metawoo mega" stays where a stranger first meets the project, the README's first sentence, and nowhere in the running interface.
